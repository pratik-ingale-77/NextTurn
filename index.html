Student

How NextTurn Works

🍔

Order Online

Select available food items from the college canteen menu and place your order from your classroom.

🎟️

Digital Token

After staff verifies your payment, you receive a digital token number for your order.

📱

Track Your Order

Track your token, current serving number and order status until your food is ready.
